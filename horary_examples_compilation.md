## PART 1: Conceptual Tajaka Yoga Examples
*Source: Sri Neelakanta's Prasna Tantra (Pages 108–111)*

These examples illustrate the core Tajaka Yogas using hypothetical planetary longitudes and coordinate charts.

### 1. Ithasala (Muthasila) Yoga (Applying Aspect)
* **Concept**: A faster-moving planet with a lesser longitude is behind a slower-moving planet in longitude and is within their combined Deepthamsas (orbs of operation).
* **Illustration**:
  - **Sun**: Aries 2° (Faster planet)
  - **Mars**: Aries 16° (Slower planet)
  - **Orb and Aspect**: The difference in longitude is 14° (applying conjunction/square/trine). Because it is within the deepthamsas (Sun: 15°, Mars: 8°), the Sun is heading to conjoin Mars. This represents an applying aspect (Ithasala), signifying that the event will happen.
  - **Poorna (Complete) Ithasala**: If the longitude difference is less than $1^\circ 0'$, the yoga is complete and immediate success is indicated.

### 2. Easarapha (Musaripha) Yoga (Separating Aspect)
* **Concept**: A faster-moving planet has passed the slower-moving planet by 1° or more.
* **Illustration**:
  - **Mercury**: Virgo 15° (Faster planet)
  - **Venus**: Virgo 14° (Slower planet)
  - **Orb and Aspect**: Mercury has crossed Venus and is separating by $1^\circ$. This represents Easarapha Yoga. It is an unfavorable combination, indicating that the query will fail, or the objective will slip away from the querent.

### 3. Nakta Yoga (Fast Translation of Light)
* **Concept**: Lagnapathi (Lagna Lord) and Karyesa (Query Lord) have no mutual aspect, but a faster-moving planet (usually the Moon) is placed between them and aspects both, translating the light.
* **Illustration**:
  - **Ascendant**: Virgo (Lagna Lord is **Mercury**, posited in **Leo 10°**)
  - **7th House**: Pisces (7th Lord is **Jupiter**, posited in **Pisces 12°**)
  - **Aspect Status**: No aspect exists between Mercury (in Leo) and Jupiter (in Pisces).
  - **Intermediary**: **Moon** is posited in **Taurus 8°**. The Moon (faster than both) aspects Mercury in Leo and Jupiter in Pisces. The Moon translates the light from Mercury to Jupiter.
  - **Astrological Verdict**: The objective will be achieved through the assistance/mediation of a third party represented by the Moon.

### 4. Yamaya Yoga (Slow Translation of Light)
* **Concept**: Lagnapathi and Karyesa have no mutual aspect, but a slower-moving planet is placed in between them and aspects both, transferring the light.
* **Illustration**:
  - **Lagna Lord**: **Venus** posited at **12°**
  - **10th Lord**: **Moon** posited at **8°**
  - **Aspect Status**: No aspect exists between Venus and the Moon.
  - **Intermediary**: **Jupiter** is posited in **Cancer 10°**. Jupiter is slower than both Venus and the Moon, but aspects both of them.
  - **Astrological Verdict**: Jupiter transfers the light from the faster Moon to the slower Venus. The objective (profession/status) will be secured through an authority figure, judge, or elder represented by Jupiter.

### 5. Kamboola Yoga (Moon Coordination)
* **Concept**: Lagnapathi and Karyesa are in Ithasala Yoga, and the Moon is also in Ithasala with one or both of them.
* **Illustration**:
  - **Lagna Lord**: **Venus** posited in **Pisces 12°** (Exalted)
  - **Karyesa**: **Jupiter** posited in **Cancer 14°** (Exalted)
  - **Aspect Status**: Venus and Jupiter are in Ithasala Yoga.
  - **Moon**: Posited in **Taurus 10°** (Exalted). The Moon is in Ithasala with both Venus and Jupiter.
  - **Astrological Verdict**: This represents a *Poorna Kamboola Yoga* of the highest order (*Uttamottama*), as all three planets are exalted. It guarantees outstanding, rapid, and complete success.

* **Significators**:
  - **Lagnapathi (Lagna Lord)**: Venus.
  - **Dhanapathi (2nd Lord - Wealth)**: Mercury.
* **Key Combinations**:
  - Lords of the Ascendant (Venus) and 2nd (Mercury) are in conjunction.
  - The lord of the 2nd (Mercury) is exalted in the 5th (Virgo/Libra junction; B.V. Raman notes Mercury's exaltation strength).

  - Lord of the 7th Mars (signifying partner/wife) is in Ithasala with both Lagnapathi Venus and 2nd lord Mercury.
  - Slower-moving Jupiter (lord of 11th - gains) is in Yamaya Yoga with the 2nd lord.
* **Prediction**: The querent would marry, receive financial assistance/dowry through marriage, and with this capital, do commission agency business ( brokerage - 5th house) and become fairly well-to-do.
* **Timing**: Mercury and Venus are approximately $2^\circ$ apart, signifying about 2 years.

#### 3. Actual Outcome
The querent married within a year, received a small dowry, started a commission agency business arranging the sale of houses/estates, and became fairly rich.
