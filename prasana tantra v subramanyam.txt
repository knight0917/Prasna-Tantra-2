PREFACE

Knowledge is power and, as admired Lord Tennyson puts it, a wise man ought:

“To follow knowledge like a sinking star,
Beyond the utmost bound of human thought”.

Astrology is rightly termed as one of the Shadangas—the six works auxiliary to the Veda. It will not be too much to say that this branch of knowledge is as necessary to civilization as any other branch, because lack of astrological knowledge means deficiency in the advancement of man.

The present work—Prasna Sastra—is one of the branches of Jyotisha Sastra and is condensed in, as the title of the book implies, 56 Slokas. The author Prithuyasas is really a worthy son of that celebrated astronomer Varahamihira. All aspects pertaining to “Query” have been very cleverly handled in this short work.

The author presumes a preliminary knowledge of Astrology and it is understood that the reader has mastered the first two chapters of Brihat Jataka.

Prithuyasas has maintained the line of thought of his father. The first line of this work starts with a prostration to the Sun—the beginner of everything.

I take this opportunity to thank my friend Mr. M. Ramakrishna Bhat, M.A., Lecturer in Sanskrit and Superintendent of Indian Languages, St. Joseph’s College, Bangalore, for the great help he rendered to me in bringing out this work.

Basavangudi,
23rd December 1940.

V. SUBRAHMANYA SASTRI


INTRODUCTION

Prasna Sastra or Horary Astrology is a branch of Astrology which enables one to answer questions relating to matters of life by judging the horoscope cast for the moment at which a query is put.

The science of Prasna has occupied a very important place in Indian Astrology. It has been elaborately dealt with by ancient sages and later writers.

Among the important works on this subject, Shatpanchasika of Prithuyasas occupies a prominent place. Though brief in extent, it contains valuable principles for judging questions connected with success, failure, disease, travel, theft, recovery of lost articles, marriage, children and several other matters.

The author, Prithuyasas, was the son of the famous Varahamihira and inherited the genius and scholarship of his father.

The present edition contains the Sanskrit text, English translation and explanatory notes intended to make the work useful to students of Astrology.


ADHYAYA I

GENERAL PRINCIPLES

Sloka 1

Prithuyasas, the son of Varahamihira, after saluting the Sun, writes this work on Horary Astrology for the benefit of the world.

The work deals with the principles relating to queries and their results. The science helps determine success or failure, gain or loss, prosperity or adversity, through the examination of Lagna, Hora, Drekkana, Navamsa and other divisions.

The author begins with an invocation to the Sun, according to the custom followed by great writers before commencing any sacred or scientific work.


Sloka 2

Transfer from one place, prosperity, absence from one’s residence, and return are to be determined respectively from the Lagna, the 4th house, the 10th house, and the 7th house.

The astrologer should carefully judge these houses in relation to the question asked.


Sloka 3

Whichever house is occupied or aspected by benefics prospers. Houses influenced by malefics suffer damage or adversity.

This rule applies both to birth horoscopes and horary charts.


Sloka 4

If benefics occupy the Lagna, or if the Navamsa rising belongs to benefic signs, success may be predicted.

If malefics influence the Lagna, failure or obstruction may result.

Mixed influences indicate success after difficulty.

Sloka 5

If the Moon, being full and strong, occupies the Lagna and is aspected by Jupiter or Venus at the time of query, recovery of the lost article should be predicted.

Likewise, if a strong benefic occupies the 11th house, the missing article may be regained.


Sloka 6

If a planet occupying its own Navamsa aspects its own Navamsa in the Lagna, the 5th or the 9th house, the subject of the query concerns a mineral (Dhatu).

If the influencing planet occupies another planet’s Navamsa, the question concerns living beings (Jeeva).

If vegetation or roots are indicated, the matter relates to Mula (plants or rooted things).


Sloka 7

In odd signs, the nine Navamsas, in regular order, represent mineral, root, and living beings, repeated three times.

In even signs, the order is reversed.

By carefully examining the Navamsa and sign involved, the astrologer can determine the nature of the object asked about.

Thus ends Adhyaya I
(General Principles)

ADHYAYA II

MARCHING AND RETURNING

Sloka 1

If any of the fixed signs—Vrishabha (Taurus), Simha (Leo), Vrischika (Scorpio), or Kumbha (Aquarius)—rises at the time of query, acquisition of position or stability may be predicted.

There will be neither movement nor return; neither death nor loss; neither cure of disease nor defeat.

Fixed signs indicate stability and lack of movement in the matter under consideration.


Sloka 2

If movable signs rise, the reverse should be predicted.

If dual signs rise, mixed results should be expected.

If benefics aspect the Lagna or the Moon, success may be expected; if malefics influence them, failure may result.


Sloka 3

If malefics occupy the 5th and 6th houses at the time of query, the enemy will retreat midway and return to his own place.

If malefics occupy the 4th house, the enemy suffers defeat and withdraws.


Sloka 4

If Meena (Pisces), Vrischika (Scorpio), Kumbha (Aquarius), or Kataka (Cancer) happens to occupy the 4th house, the enemy retreats with his army or followers.

The astrologer should judge the sign and house carefully before predicting the result.


Sloka 5

If a movable sign rises and benefics occupy it, success should be predicted for one commencing a journey or undertaking.

If malefics occupy the sign, unfavourable results may follow.

Even if a fixed sign rises, benefic influence can bring favourable outcomes.


Sloka 6

If the Lagna is movable and the Moon occupies a fixed sign, the enemy will not come.

If the Lagna is fixed and the Moon occupies a movable sign, the enemy will arrive.

The relation between the sign rising and the Moon’s sign should be carefully judged to determine movement or arrival.


Sloka 7

If the Lagna is fixed and the Moon occupies a dual sign, the enemy, even after travelling a long distance, will return home.

The undertaking may begin but may not reach completion.


Sloka 8

If the Lagna is dual and the Moon occupies a movable sign, the enemy comes halfway and then returns.

If the Lagna is movable and the Moon occupies a dual sign afflicted by malefics, conflict may arise before retreat.


Sloka 9

If a movable sign rises and is occupied by the Sun, Saturn, Mercury, or Venus, immediate movement or march should be predicted.

But if the planet, except the Sun, is retrograde, withdrawal or delay may occur.


Sloka 10

If the Lagna is a fixed sign and is aspected by Jupiter and Saturn, nothing definite should be predicted regarding the enemy’s arrival or departure.

If malefics occupy the 3rd, 5th, and 6th houses, conflict with the enemy is likely.

If malefics occupy the 4th house, the enemy retreats to his own place.


Sloka 11

If the Sun and Moon occupy the 4th house from the Lagna at the time of query, the enemy’s army will not arrive.

If Mercury, Jupiter, or Venus occupy the 4th house, the enemy’s army will come quickly.


Sloka 12

If Mesha (Aries), Dhanus (Sagittarius), Simha (Leo), or Vrishabha (Taurus) rises at the time of query, the enemy retreats to his own place.

If planets strongly influence the 4th house, the result should be judged accordingly.


Sloka 13

If Saturn or Jupiter occupies a fixed Lagna, the enemy, though having started, will remain where he is and not proceed.

If a movable sign rises and the Sun or Jupiter occupies it, the enemy’s arrival should be predicted.


Sloka 14

The strongest planet should be identified and the number of houses it is removed from the Lagna should be counted.

The return of a person gone away may be predicted in as many months as the number indicated by that interval.


Sloka 15

The period of return should be modified according to the Navamsa occupied by the strongest planet.

If the Navamsa belongs to a movable sign, the indicated time remains unchanged.

If it belongs to a fixed sign, the period should be doubled.

If it belongs to a dual sign, the period should be trebled.

Thus the astrologer should carefully consider the nature of the Navamsa before fixing the exact time of return.


Sloka 16

Some teachers state that the return of one who has gone away—especially for battle or travel—may be expected when the lord of the 7th house from the Prasna Lagna becomes retrograde.

Retrogression signifies turning back or reversal and therefore indicates return.


Sloka 17

If benefics occupy the 2nd, 3rd, and 5th houses from the Lagna, victory should be predicted for the querist.

If malefics occupy these houses, defeat should be predicted.

This rule is especially useful in matters concerning war, litigation, and disputes.


Sloka 18

If benefics occupy the Lagna and malefics occupy the 7th house, the querist becomes victorious.

If the reverse occurs, the enemy gains success.

The Lagna represents the querist and the 7th house represents the opponent; hence their comparative strength determines the outcome.


Thus ends Adhyaya II
(Marching and Returning)


ADHYAYA III

SUCCESS AND DEFEAT

Sloka 1

If benefic planets occupy the Lagna and strong planets influence the 10th house, success should be predicted in the undertaking.

If malefics occupy these positions, the desired object may not be attained.

The strength of the Lagna, its lord, and the 10th house should be carefully judged in all matters relating to success.


Sloka 2

If the lord of the Lagna is strong, associated with benefics, and the Moon is favourably situated, success becomes certain.

If the lord of the Lagna is weak, combust, debilitated, or afflicted by malefics, failure may result.

Planetary strength should always be judged before giving a final prediction.


Sloka 3

If benefics occupy or aspect the 11th house, gain and fulfilment of desires should be predicted.

If malefics influence the 11th house, gains may be delayed or denied.

The 11th house represents gains, fulfilment, and realization of objectives.


Sloka 4

If the lord of the Lagna occupies a Kendra (angular house) or Trikona (trinal house) and joins benefics, success should confidently be declared.

If the lord of the Lagna occupies the 6th, 8th, or 12th house and suffers affliction, obstacles and failure may arise.


Sloka 5

If benefics occupy the Lagna, the 7th, or the 10th house, success and happiness should be predicted.

If cruel planets occupy these places, trouble, delay, and loss may arise.

Benefic influences upon angular houses help smooth completion of undertakings.


Sloka 6

If the Moon is associated with benefics or occupies favourable houses, success should be predicted.

If the Moon is afflicted, waning, debilitated, or joined with malefics, disappointment may arise.

The Moon is an important factor in horary astrology and should always be carefully judged.


Sloka 7

If Jupiter or Venus aspects the Lagna or its lord, favourable results should be expected.

If Saturn, Mars, or the Sun heavily afflict the Lagna, setbacks and difficulties may arise.


Sloka 8

If the Lagna, Moon, and the lord of the matter under consideration are all strong, success is assured.

If one among them is weak, moderate success may occur.

If all are weak or afflicted, failure should be predicted.


Sloka 9

When benefics occupy the 11th house and malefics remain weak, gain should be predicted.

If malefics dominate the house of gains, acquisition becomes difficult or delayed.

Sloka 10

If planets connected with the matter under consideration occupy exaltation, own sign, or friendly signs, success should be expected.

If they occupy debilitation or inimical signs, failure, suffering, or delay may result.

The dignity and strength of planets should always be considered before arriving at a conclusion.


Sloka 11

If benefics occupy Kendras (angular houses) and Trikonas (trinal houses), while malefics occupy Upachaya houses (3rd, 6th, 10th, and 11th), success should be confidently predicted.

Such placements strengthen effort and diminish obstacles.


Sloka 12

If benefics and malefics both influence the principal houses, mixed results should be predicted.

Success may come, but only after obstacles, anxiety, or delay.

The astrologer should carefully weigh the comparative strength of the planets before deciding the final outcome.


Thus ends Adhyaya III
(Success and Defeat)


ADHYAYA IV

VICTORY AND DEFEAT

Sloka 1

If the lord of the Lagna is strong and favourably placed while the lord of the 7th house is weak, victory should be predicted for the querist.

If the reverse is the case, defeat should be declared.

The Lagna represents the querist and the 7th house represents the opponent. Their comparative strength decides the outcome of disputes, litigation, war, or rivalry.


Sloka 2

If benefics occupy the Lagna and the Moon is strong, success and happiness should be predicted.

If the Moon is afflicted by malefics and the Lagna suffers affliction, anxiety and failure may arise.

The Moon indicates mental condition and progress of the matter asked about.


Sloka 3

When benefics occupy Kendras and Trikonas, prosperity and success follow.

If malefics dominate these places, loss and suffering may result.

Mixed influences indicate success only after struggle and delay.


Sloka 4

If Mars occupies the 6th house and is strong, the enemy suffers defeat.

If Saturn occupies the same place, prolonged difficulties and obstacles may occur.

The 6th house represents enemies, competition, and resistance.


Sloka 5

If benefics occupy the 11th house, gain and fulfilment of desires should be expected.

Affliction to the 11th house causes delay or disappointment in results.

Sloka 6

If the lord of the Lagna and the Moon are both strong and associated with benefics, success in the undertaking should be predicted.

If both are weak, afflicted, or associated with malefics, the undertaking may fail or suffer severe obstacles.


Sloka 7

A planet occupying exaltation, own sign, or a friendly sign produces favourable results.

A planet occupying debility or an inimical sign weakens the matter and causes hindrances.

The astrologer should carefully judge planetary dignity before predicting the result.


Sloka 8

If benefics influence the house connected with the matter under consideration, favourable results should be predicted.

If malefics influence that house, loss, fear, obstruction, or destruction may result.

The house concerned, its lord, and planetary aspects should all be carefully examined.


Thus ends Adhyaya IV
(Victory and Defeat)


ADHYAYA V

LOSS, THEFT, AND RECOVERY

Sloka 1

If benefics occupy the Lagna and the Moon is strong at the time of query, recovery of the lost article should be predicted.

If malefics occupy the Lagna and afflict the Moon, recovery becomes difficult or impossible.

The Lagna and the Moon are important factors in determining the recovery of lost property.


Sloka 2

If the lord of the 2nd house is strong and associated with benefics, lost property may be regained.

If the lord of the 2nd house is weak or afflicted, permanent loss may result.

The 2nd house signifies wealth, possessions, and valuables.


Sloka 3

If benefics occupy the 4th house, the lost object remains within the house or nearby.

If malefics occupy the 4th house, the object may have been taken elsewhere.

The 4th house represents home, residence, and concealed places.


Sloka 4

If a movable sign rises, the stolen article may have been carried to a distant place.

If a fixed sign rises, the object remains nearby.

If a dual sign rises, the object is at a moderate distance.

The nature of the sign rising helps determine the location of the missing article.


Sloka 5

If the Moon aspects the Lagna or joins benefic planets, recovery of the lost property should be predicted.

If the Moon joins malefics or becomes weak, delay or non-recovery may result.

The Moon plays an important role in deciding the restoration of missing articles.


Sloka 6

If the 7th house is afflicted by malefics, the thief may be identified and apprehended.

If benefics influence the 7th house, peaceful recovery or compromise may occur.

The 7th house is used in horary astrology to judge the thief or opposing person.


Sloka 7

If benefics occupy the 11th house, restoration of property and gain should be predicted.

Affliction to the 11th house causes delay or difficulty in recovery.


Sloka 8

If the lord of the Lagna aspects the 2nd or the 11th house, recovery of the lost article becomes likely.

The astrologer should judge the houses of wealth, gain, and the Moon together before reaching a conclusion.


Thus ends Adhyaya V
(Loss, Theft, and Recovery)


ADHYAYA VI

TRAVEL, RETURN, AND ABSENCE

Sloka 1

If a movable sign rises at the time of query and benefics occupy or aspect the Lagna, successful travel should be predicted.

If malefics influence the Lagna, obstacles, delay, or danger may arise during the journey.

The nature of the rising sign and planetary influence determine the success or failure of travel.


Sloka 2

If benefics occupy the 7th house, safe return from travel may be expected.

If malefics afflict the 7th house, delay, danger, or difficulty in returning may arise.

The 7th house plays an important role in judging return from distant places.


Sloka 3

If the lord of the Lagna joins the lord of the 7th house, reunion or return becomes likely.

If the two are separated or mutually afflicted, distance and delay are indicated.


Sloka 4

When the Moon occupies a movable sign and receives benefic aspects, travel succeeds quickly.

If the Moon is weak or afflicted, interruptions or hardships may arise during the journey.


Sloka 5

If benefics occupy the 9th house, fortune and protection accompany the traveller.

If malefics occupy or aspect the 9th house, danger, misfortune, or delay may arise.

The 9th house signifies long journeys, luck, and divine favour.


Sloka 6

If the 4th house is strong and influenced by benefics, safe return to home should be predicted.

Affliction to the 4th house delays return or causes domestic troubles.


Sloka 7

If Saturn strongly influences the Lagna or Moon, prolonged delay in travel or return should be expected.

If Jupiter influences them, protection and eventual success may be predicted.


Sloka 8

If benefics influence the Lagna, Moon, and 7th house together, the traveller returns safely and benefits from the journey.

If malefics dominate these factors, obstacles, fear, or disappointment may arise.


Thus ends Adhyaya VI
(Travel, Return, and Absence)

ADHYAYA VII

MISCELLANEOUS

Sloka 1

If Saturn be posited in an odd house (3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th or 11th) from the Lagna, the birth of a male should be predicted. Otherwise, the birth will be of a girl.

In the query for a marriage, if Saturn occupy any one of the even houses, one should predict the acquisition of a bride; if otherwise, the contrary should be predicted.

This chapter deals with “Miscellaneous matters”. At the outset the author gives a clue to determine whether a woman who is pregnant will be delivered of a male or a female child; and whether a person who is in search of a bride will get one or not.

When Saturn occupies an odd house from the Lagna at query, i.e., the 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th and 11th, it should be predicted that the querist will have a son.

Further, if Saturn occupies any one of the even houses from the Lagna, i.e., the 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th and 12th, a person who is in quest of a bride will get one.


Sloka 2

If, at the time of query, the Moon be posited in the 3rd, 5th, 7th, 11th or the 6th house from the Prasna Lagna and be aspected by Jupiter, the Sun and Mercury, the marriage of the querist will come to pass.

The same should be predicted when benefic planets occupy the Trikona and Kendra houses reckoned from the Prasna Lagna.

The author now treats of the determination of marriage. If the Moon occupying the 3rd, 5th, 7th, 11th or 6th house from the Lagna be aspected by Jupiter, the Sun and Mercury, the wedding would take place. If benefics occupy the Trikona and Kendra places, the querist will be married.


Sloka 3

If Venus and Saturn be posited in the 7th house reckoned respectively from the Moon and the Sun, or in the 4th and the 8th houses from the Lagna, or in the 2nd and 3rd houses from the Lagna, one should predict the advent of rain, if it be the rainy season.

In this Sloka, the author gives a clue to determine rain in the monsoon time.

Sloka 4

If Venus and Mercury occupy the 2nd, 3rd or 5th house from the Lagna and Jupiter occupies a Kendra or Trikona, one should predict the birth of a male child.

If the Moon and Venus are strong and occupy even signs or houses, the birth of a female child should be predicted.

The author gives combinations to determine whether the child in the womb will be male or female. Strength of benefics and the nature of signs should be carefully examined.


Sloka 5

If benefics occupy the Lagna and the 5th house while the lord of the 5th is strong, safe delivery of the child should be predicted.

If the 5th house is afflicted by malefics and the Moon is weak, danger or trouble to the child may be indicated.

The astrologer should carefully judge the 5th house, its lord, and the Moon in questions concerning pregnancy and childbirth.


Sloka 6

If benefics occupy Kendras and Trikonas and the Lagna is strong, happiness and prosperity should be predicted.

If malefics occupy the principal houses, anxiety, delay, or disappointment may result.

The result of any undertaking should always be judged by the comparative strength of benefics and malefics.


Sloka 7

If benefics occupy the 2nd, 7th and 11th houses, acquisition of wealth, marriage, and fulfilment of desires should be predicted.

If malefics occupy these houses, obstruction, disappointment, or delay may arise.

The 2nd house signifies wealth, the 7th marriage and partnership, and the 11th gains and realization of desires.


Sloka 8

If the lord of the house connected with the query is strong, occupies favourable places, and is associated with benefics, success should be predicted.

If weak, afflicted, combust, or joined with malefics, failure or obstacles may arise.

This is a general rule applicable to all kinds of horary questions.


Sloka 9

If benefics influence the Moon and Lagna, peace and happiness should be expected.

If malefics influence them, sorrow, fear, or difficulty may arise.

The Moon and Lagna should always be carefully examined in every Prasna.


Thus ends Adhyaya VII
(Miscellaneous)


Sloka 4

If benefic planets occupy the 3rd, 2nd, 1st, 4th, 7th and 10th houses reckoned from the Prasna Lagna and these houses happen to be also watery signs, and if it also be the bright half of the month, one should predict rain. The same will be the case if the Moon happens to be in the Lagna identical with a watery sign.

In this sloka, he discusses the determination of rain at the time of a query. The watery signs are Kataka, Meena, Makara and Kumbha.


Sloka 5

If the (a) Varga that is rising at the time of query be a masculine one, (b) be strong (c) and be aspected by male planets, the issue to be born will be a male one (d). If the said Varga be a female one and be aspected by female planets, the issue will be a female. If the Prasna Lagna be occupied by Mercury, the woman (e) concerned should be declared as enceinte.

In this sloka the author gives us a clue to determine the sex of the child which the pregnant woman is to be delivered of, and in the case of a query regarding pregnancy or otherwise, whether a particular woman may be declared as pregnant.

(a) The term Varga has already been defined in page 7, supra.

(b) The male and female signs are assorted in the work thus—Mesha, Mithuna, Simha, Tula, Dhanus and Kumbha are called male signs, while the remaining six—Vrishabha, Kataka, Kanya, Vrischika, Makara and Meena are known as female signs.

(c) The Lagna will be strong when occupied or aspected by its lord, Mercury or Jupiter. Otherwise, it will not become powerful even when aspected by all the other planets.

(d) The masculine, feminine and neutral characteristics of planets are given as: Mercury and Saturn are eunuchs, the Moon and Venus are females, and the rest, males. When a Varga of a male Rasi is rising at the time, and is aspected by a male planet and when the Lagna is powerful, a boy will be born.

(e) The query should be understood in the case of a woman suspected to be pregnant.

If the Lagna be a female sign, aspected by female planets and strong, the issue will be female. If neutral influences predominate, or mixed influences are present, the result should be judged after carefully weighing planetary strength.


Sloka 6

If benefic planets occupy the 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th and 10th houses from the Lagna and the Moon be strong, one should predict prosperity, happiness, and success in undertakings.

If malefics occupy these places, anxiety, trouble, or disappointment may arise.

The author concludes by stating that benefic influences on important houses confer happiness and success, while malefic influences create obstacles and suffering.


Thus ends Adhyaya VII
(Miscellaneous)


ADHYAYA VIII

ON RAINFALL

Sloka 1

If benefic planets occupy watery signs and the Moon is strong and associated with benefics, rain should be predicted.

If malefics occupy dry signs and afflict the Moon, scarcity of rain should be declared.

The author now takes up the question of rainfall and weather indications based on planetary positions at the time of query.


Sloka 2

If Venus occupies a watery sign and Jupiter aspects the Moon, copious rainfall may be expected.

If Saturn or Mars afflicts the Moon, scanty rain or delay in rainfall may occur.

The astrologer should particularly observe the Moon, Venus, and watery signs in questions relating to rain.


Sloka 3

If benefics occupy Kendras and Trikonas and the Lagna is strong, favourable seasonal conditions should be expected.

If malefics dominate the principal houses, drought, irregular weather, or disturbances may arise.


Sloka 4

If Venus and Saturn occupy the 7th houses respectively from the Moon and the Sun, or if they occupy the 4th and 8th houses from the Lagna, rain should be predicted during the rainy season.

Likewise, if Venus and Mercury occupy the 2nd, 3rd, or 5th houses from the Lagna and Jupiter occupies a Kendra or Trikona, rainfall should be expected.

The author gives several combinations for determining rain during the monsoon period. The influence of benefics and watery signs should be carefully examined.


Sloka 5

If benefic planets occupy watery signs and the Moon is strong, abundant rainfall should be predicted.

If malefics dominate dry signs and afflict the Moon, little or no rain may occur.

The astrologer should judge the nature of signs, the strength of the Moon, and benefic influences before arriving at a conclusion.


Thus ends Adhyaya VIII
(On Rainfall)
